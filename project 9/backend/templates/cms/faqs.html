Rooftop FAQs & Dining Policies

Essential guest guidelines regarding dress code, terrace access, elevator express lines, and cancellation policies.

What is the dress code for Level 54?

Smart elegant attire is strictly requested for all evening dinner services and horizon lounge access. We kindly ask gentlemen to wear tailored trousers and collared shirts. Athletic apparel, beach footwear, and baseball caps are not permitted past 5:00 PM.

How are terrace tables handled during adverse weather?

Our open-air terrace features automated retractable pergolas and architectural radiant heaters. In the event of high winds or extreme weather, terrace reservations are automatically transitioned to our panoramic indoor sky lounge with priority window placement.

How far in advance can table reservations be booked?

Sunset terrace and window tables open for booking 30 calendar days in advance at 9:00 AM EST. Private horizon suites and full buyout inquiries may be arranged up to twelve months in advance through our events concierge.
