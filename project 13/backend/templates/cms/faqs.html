❓ Dining FAQs Manager

Frequently asked questions regarding late-night seating, spice levels, walk-ins, and soju pairings.

Do you accept walk-ins after midnight?

Yes! While advance reservations are recommended for peak 19:00 - 22:00 dinner hours, late-night pocha tables (23:00 - 04:00) operate on a first-come, first-served basis.

Can the spice level be adjusted?

Absolutely. Our tteokbokki and chicken can be prepared from Level 0 (Mild Honey Butter / Garlic Soy) up to Level 3 (Fire Gochujang Hellfire).

Are halal-friendly or vegetarian options available?

Yes, we offer crispy vegetable tempura, vegan kimchi jeon made without fish sauce, and our chicken is 100% certified halal-sourced.
