Concierge & Guest Arrival FAQs

Guidelines for yacht tender arrivals, dress codes, private dining minimums, and dietary protocol.

What is the protocol for private yacht arrival?

Patrons arriving by yacht may coordinate tender mooring directly with our pier concierge via VHF Channel 68 or by notifying our Maitre D' 30 minutes in advance.

What is the dress code at Villa Riviera?

Riviera Smart Elegant. For Daylight Lunch, linen shirts and tailored resort wear are welcomed. For Twilight Dinner, tailored jackets are recommended for gentlemen; beachwear and sportswear are not permitted.

How are dietary sensitivities accommodated?

With 48 hours notice, Chef Antoine Laurent curates specialized tasting journeys accommodating gluten sensitivity, shellfish allergies, or pescatarian diets without compromise on flavor.
