The Culinary Vision

Charred & Prime is an unapologetic temple to carnivorous luxury. Smoked black oak, flamed stone, amber illumination, glass-walled dry-aging lockers, and the primal theater of Argentine wood-fired grills create an unforgettable night.

01

Himalayan Salt Dry-Aging

Custom glass lockers lined with pink salt bricks, aging prime beef from 35 to 90 days.

02

Parilla Fire Theater

Hardwood coals and white oak embers searing prime cuts at 1,200°F for perfect caramelization.

03

Rare Whiskey Vault

300+ single malt scotches, rare Japanese reserves, and pre-prohibition bourbon rarities.
